# Research directions

These are potential extensions, not completed results. Executed studies and their evidence are indexed in the [repository overview](../README.md).

- **Quantum chemistry:** quantify basis, active-space and ansatz errors separately against matched classical references; add finite-shot and noise/resource studies.
- **Molecular learning:** evaluate authorized measurement records against the frozen ADMET preparation, scaffold-separated classical baselines and readout ablations. Keep proprietary results confidential.
- **Quantum optimization:** test broader allocation neighborhoods with verified headroom and compare sampling methods under explicit computational budgets.
- **Reservoir computing:** extend to additional years and regions using as-of data, repeated chronological evaluation and finite-shot controls.
- **Quantum algorithms:** examine unknown-success amplification, iterative phase estimation and arithmetic with fault-tolerant rotation costs.

Any extension should state its scientific question, reference comparison, evaluation boundary and resource assumptions before results are interpreted. The existing studies do not establish quantum advantage or general predictive superiority.
